Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Data We Collect

To provide the Service, we collect the information you give us directly, including:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, and password
  • Expense records you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions
  • Basic usage information, such as when you sign in and how you interact with the app

We do not knowingly collect sensitive financial account numbers, bank credentials, or payment card details — Spendly is a manual expense tracker and does not connect to your bank accounts.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including displaying your expense summaries and insights
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Improve and troubleshoot the Service based on how it is used
  • Communicate with you about your account or important changes to the Service

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use your expense data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our application database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable period.

Third Party Services

We may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting, infrastructure, and analytics. These providers only process data as needed to perform their services on our behalf and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. Spendly does not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the support channels provided within the app.
